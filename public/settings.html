How to configure:
  1. Open FINAI/.env in your workspace.
  2. Set GOOGLE_SHEETS_WEBHOOK_URL to your deployed Apps Script web app URL, or provide GOOGLE_SHEETS_SPREADSHEET_ID, GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_EMAIL, and GOOGLE_PRIVATE_KEY.
  3. Restart the server. All evaluations will automatically append as rows!
